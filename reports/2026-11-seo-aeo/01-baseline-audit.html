Summary

The site is fast, readable on phones and honestly written. What it lacks is the plumbing machines rely on: no robots.txt, no sitemap, no canonical URLs, no social share tags and no structured data. As a result, Google and AI answer engines have to guess who Kay is, what the books cost and which URL counts. Nothing is broken for visitors except one visual bug on the Library page.

Findings: 0 critical, 9 high, 15 medium and 14 low (failed checks grouped by type, plus 15 findings from Lighthouse, screenshots and manual review).

The supplied patch is already applied and verified on branch seo-aeo-fixes. Re-running the same audit on that branch gives 94/100 (A), up from 68. It fixes robots.txt, the sitemap, canonicals, Open Graph, author attribution and structured data. The items it doesn't cover are listed as still open in the after-report.

How the scores work

Every score comes from tools/seo_audit.py, which you can run again at any time and get the same result for the same site. It crawls the site from the home page and the sitemap, follows every internal link, and runs 49 defined checks. Each check belongs to one category and has a fixed severity:

SeverityWeightMeaning
Critical5Stops a page being found, loaded or bought
High3Directly harms indexing, rich results, accessibility or the main image load
Medium2Weakens how the page is understood, shared or used
Low1Polish and best practice

Category score = weight of passed checks ÷ weight of all checks run, × 100. A check runs once per page it applies to, so a problem on six pages counts six times. Overall is the same sum across all categories. Grades: A 90+, B 80–89, C 70–79, D 60–69, F below 60.

Checks the audit cannot complete (for example checkout pages behind bot protection) are listed as unverified and left out of the score rather than counted as passes. Lighthouse figures are separate, measured evidence: Lighthouse 12.8.2, mobile profile with simulated throttling, median of 3 runs per page. Findings from Lighthouse, screenshots and manual review are listed with their evidence but are not part of the score, so the score stays repeatable.

Reproduce: python3 tools/seo_audit.py --base https://kayjoyflowers.online · local copy: python3 tools/seo_audit.py --root . · Lighthouse: node tools/lighthouse_runs.mjs --runs 3

About the reference method

This follows the loop in Mika Reyes's How to audit your website for SEO and AEO with AI: a teaching-first audit (problem → principle → fix), implementation, a re-audit with the same method, verification on the live site, and a publishing standard so new pages start clean. One change from the article: its letter grades were "directional". Here every score comes from a script, so it can be repeated and checked.

Generated by tools/build_report.py from reports/2026-11-seo-aeo/data/. No rankings, traffic or AI-citation figures are reported, because none were measured.
