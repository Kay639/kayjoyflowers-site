What is verified, what is expected, what can't be measured yet

Expected benefit (not measurable yet)
  • Faster, more reliable indexing from the sitemap, canonical URLs and robots.txt.
  • Richer, page-specific previews when links are shared in Instagram DMs, Facebook, iMessage, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X.

Can't be measured yet
  • Rankings, impressions, clicks or AI citations: no change can be measured until the site is live and Google Search Console has data (allow 2–6 weeks after deploy).

  • Beacons checkout pages: blocked to automated visitors (Cloudflare), so they need a manual click-through.
  • Stripe payment link prices: the connected Stripe account isn't the one that holds the scan links, so prices couldn't be read by API. The links return HTTP 200.

How the scores work

Every score comes from tools/seo_audit.py, which you can run again at any time and get the same result for the same site. It crawls the site from the home page and the sitemap, follows every internal link, and runs 49 defined checks. Each check belongs to one category and has a fixed severity:

SeverityWeightMeaning
Critical5Stops a page being found, loaded or bought
High3Directly harms indexing, rich results, accessibility or the main image load
Medium2Weakens how the page is understood, shared or used
Low1Polish and best practice

Category score = weight of passed checks ÷ weight of all checks run, × 100. A check runs once per page it applies to, so a problem on six pages counts six times. Overall is the same sum across all categories. Grades: A 90+, B 80–89, C 70–79, D 60–69, F below 60.

Checks the audit cannot complete (for example checkout pages behind bot protection) are listed as unverified and left out of the score rather than counted as passes. Lighthouse figures are separate, measured evidence: Lighthouse 12.8.2, mobile profile with simulated throttling, median of 3 runs per page. Findings from Lighthouse, screenshots and manual review are listed with their evidence but are not part of the score, so the score stays repeatable.
