Questions

Before you begin.

Do I need to be in New Zealand?

No. Scans are done by distance using your photo and voice recording, and the Capsule works anywhere.

What do I need to send?

A recent photo of you, a voice recording of about 15 seconds (just talk naturally), and anything you're experiencing or would like me to keep in mind. You'll do this on a private form straight after payment.

When will I hear from you?

I'll confirm I've received your form, then send your feedback once your scan is done. Members receive a new scan and new frequencies each week.

Do I send a new voice recording every week?

No. Your first recording is enough, and I use it for your scans. If you ever want a fresh reading from a new recording, you're welcome to send one.

How do I cancel the membership?

Any time, through Manage my membership (also linked in your confirmation email), or by emailing me. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. Your Capsule stays yours.

What happens to my photo, voice and information?

They're stored privately, never on this public website, and only I can see them. I use them only for your scans. See the privacy policy for how long I keep them and how to ask me to delete them.
