Who this is for

You're not broken. Your body is still bracing.

This is for women who are tired of knowing what they should do but finding themselves thrown off course by pressure, other people's expectations, self-doubt, emotional upheaval or circumstances beyond their control.

Life may be stabilising, yet the urgency, the over-functioning and the scanning for the next problem are still running. Survival kept you alive. It doesn't have to keep running your life.
