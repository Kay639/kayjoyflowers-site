Living Cosmology of Remembrance is created by Kay Joy Flowers and hosted by AI voices, Iris and Jonah. The ideas and every quotation come from Kay's published writing; the hosts never speak as her.

Who are you when you are not explaining your usefulness?

In the first episode of Series One, Who ARE You?, AI hosts Iris and Jonah talk through Kay Joy Flowers' Journal essay. They look at why a meaningful role can still become too small, what Kay means by remembrance, where this question sits among the seven gateways of A Living Cosmology of Remembrance, and the difference between a role and the rule that attaches itself to it.

Try this week: Finish the sentence “To be allowed to remain this person, I believe I have to…”, then ask whether you chose that condition.
