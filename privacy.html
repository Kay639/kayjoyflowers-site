Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information I (Kay Joy Flowers) collect when you visit kayjoyflowers.online, buy from my Beacons store, or sign in to my digital products, and what I do with it. I'm based in New Zealand and follow the Privacy Act 2020. My products are for adults aged 18 and over.

What I collect and why

  • Name and email address (at checkout): to send what you bought and your receipt. Without an email I can't deliver it.
  • What you bought, the price, date and order number (at checkout): to give you access, handle refunds and keep tax records.
  • Payment card details (at checkout): handled by Stripe only. I never see your full card number.
  • Email address and sign-in records (when you sign in to a product such as the Unfuckwithable Companion): to check you bought it and let you in.
  • Anything you write to me (when you email me): to reply.

Your writing in the Unfuckwithable Companion stays on your device. Journal entries are saved in your own browser. They aren't sent to me or anyone else. If you clear your browser data or change device, they won't come with you.

Some of this information reaches me through other services (Beacons and Stripe) rather than from you directly. This policy is my notice to you about that.

Services that handle it for me

These providers store or process information on my behalf to run my business:

  • Beacons: online store and checkout (United States).
  • Stripe: payment processing (United States and other countries).
  • Supabase: product sign-in and access records (Japan).
  • Resend: sign-in and service emails (Japan).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website (United States).
  • Google Fonts: supplies this website's fonts and receives your device's IP address (United States).

Beacons and Stripe also use some information for their own purposes, such as fraud prevention, under their own privacy policies.

Advertising: sharing purchases with Meta

When you buy from my store, my system automatically tells Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram) that a purchase was made. It sends the order value, currency, the product, an order reference, and your email address in hashed form.

Hashing turns your email into a fixed code before it's sent. It isn't anonymous: Meta compares the code with codes made from its own users' emails, and if they match, it links the purchase to that Facebook or Instagram account.

Meta uses this to:

  • measure which of my adverts lead to sales
  • improve who my adverts are shown to
  • for its own purposes, under its own terms

I don't send your name, address or payment details to Meta.

Meta is based outside New Zealand (in Ireland and the United States). It may not be required to protect your information in the same way New Zealand law does.

Opting out. Sharing happens automatically when you pay, so it can't be undone for a purchase already made. Email me, before or after you buy, and I'll add you to a "do not share" list that my system checks before sending anything to Meta, so your future purchases aren't shared. You can also manage how Meta uses your data in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

Cookies

kayjoyflowers.online sets no cookies and runs no tracking or advertising code. My Beacons store pages are run by Beacons, which uses its own cookies; see Beacons' privacy policy. Purchase reporting to Meta is sent from my systems, not from your browser, so it doesn't rely on cookies.

How long I keep it

  • Purchase records (name, email, product, price, date): 7 years, because New Zealand tax law requires it.
  • Sign-in and access records: for as long as you have access to the product. You can ask me to close your access sooner.
  • Emails you send me: until the matter is finished, unless they form part of a tax record.
  • "Do not share" requests: for as long as needed to honour them.

Meta keeps what it receives under its own policy.

Your rights

You can ask me for the personal information I hold about you, and ask me to correct it. Email me and I'll tell you my decision within 20 working days, then give you the information promptly. If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, you can complain to New Zealand's Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
