# Content opportunities (SEO and AEO)

These are ideas for future articles and page improvements, drawn only from themes already in Kay's published work. **No search volumes are given, because none were measured.** Before writing, check Google Search Console (free, once the site is verified) for the queries people already use to find the site, and pick the ideas those queries support.

Every piece must be written by Kay or approved by her, in her voice. None of this is drafted copy.

1. **Library collection descriptions.** Each coming-soon collection is listed by title only (the page has 128 words). One or two sentences per collection, in Kay's words, would give search and AI engines something to understand and quote. This is the only open item in the audit's AEO category.
2. **Fearlessly Unfuckwithable FAQ.** A draft built only from facts already on the site is waiting for approval in `reports/2026-11-seo-aeo/drafts/`.

## Rules
- One question per article, and answer it in the first two or three sentences.
- No medical or therapeutic claims. Keep the "not therapy, not medical care" framing wherever the body, the nervous system or health is discussed.
- Follow the [publishing standard](PUBLISHING-STANDARD.md) (BlogPosting markup, byline, dates, sitemap, internal link from an existing page).
